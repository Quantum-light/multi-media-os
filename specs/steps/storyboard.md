# Step: storyboard (video shows)

**Purpose.** Plan where the brand graphics go on the edited master, so M7 can render them.

**Input** (`StoryboardInput` = `ClipOutput`), plus `understand`, `cut` and `write` results.
**Output** (`StoryboardOutput`): cues on the edited timeline (lower third with the show name at 3 s, a chapter
card at every chapter after the first, up to two quote cards from claims and teachings, an end card 15 s
from the end) and the edited duration.

Deterministic, no paid call. After this step the episode waits at Review.
