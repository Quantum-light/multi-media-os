# Journeys with Time — how the clip scout chooses

Edit this file to change how clips are picked. It is sent to Claude on every run.

## The feeling
Awe, with the ground still under your feet. Every clip carries someone from their
ordinary scroll into the scale of the universe and back, a little larger inside.
Five beats: Stillness → Wonder → Vastness → Gravitas → Return ("you are made of this").
Never hype, fear, clickbait or cheap mysticism.

## What makes a great clip
- The first sentence grips on its own: a question, a reframe, or a surprising true idea.
  It must not start mid-thought ("so", "and", "which means").
- It makes complete sense with no context from the rest of the episode.
- It contains at least one line with gravitas: a sentence worth pausing on.
- It ends on a finished thought, ideally a line that lands (the Return beat).
- The science is said accurately. Flag any claim that needs checking.
- Grace's own voice, passion and metaphors are gold. Personal stories that make the
  science lived are gold.

## Lengths (choose a mix)
- short: 15–30 seconds (TikTok, Reels) — about 4 clips
- medium: 30–60 seconds (Reels, Shorts) — about 4 clips
- long: 60–90 seconds (Shorts, teaser for the full episode) — 2 or 3 clips
Total 9–12 clips. Clips must not overlap.

## Publishing order
Put the single strongest, most universal clip first. Then alternate lengths and topics so
no two neighbours feel the same. Keep the most demanding ideas for after the audience is warm.

## Scores (1–10 each)
hook, standalone, gravitas, accuracy. Reject anything under 6 on accuracy or standalone.
