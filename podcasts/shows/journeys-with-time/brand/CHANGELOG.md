# Journeys with Time — brand changelog

## v1 — 19 October 2026
- First brand kit, from brand lab round 1.
- Art direction set: atmospheric and ethereal, yet graphic. AI makes the atmosphere only; the science is drawn in code as thin burnished-gold lines with no glow.
- Approved plates: misty-horizon (different views), silk-veil (the disconnection of time), light-threads (the light and lines), deep-veil (the graphic look of spacetime), spectral-mist (frequencies of waves), breath-of-light.
- Rejected: liquid-gold (the 3D aspect) → added "no 3D-rendered objects or faces" to never-do.
- Earlier rounds rejected before the lab: neon and glow ("too bright, like neon"); photographic regal objects ("needs to feel beyond matter").
